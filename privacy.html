Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16.11.2026

In short: Klinger Scan does not collect personal data. Everything happens on your iPhone.

Camera

The camera is used only to read text on doorbell panels and parcel labels. Recognition runs entirely on your device. Photos are not saved and are never uploaded.

Your name list

The names you enter or scan are stored only on your iPhone and are deleted automatically after 24 hours. Your language, theme and colour settings are also stored only on the device.

No account, no tracking

There is no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share data.

Text recognition library

Text recognition uses Google ML Kit, which runs on the device. According to Google, ML Kit may send anonymous usage and performance statistics to Google. Images and recognised text are not sent.

Apple

Ratings, reviews and sharing use Apple’s built-in system features. Purchases, if offered, are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Support messages

If you write to us through the Support page, your message and email address are delivered to us by email through the FormSubmit service. We use them only to answer you.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: see the Support page.

Apple

Повідомлення в підтримку

Якщо ви пишете нам через сторінку підтримки, ваше повідомлення та email надходять нам електронною поштою через сервіс FormSubmit. Ми використовуємо їх лише для відповіді вам.
